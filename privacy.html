Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who uses this app

Mail Pipeline is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads messages and attachments, and adds labels. It never sends, deletes, archives or removes labels from mail.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.calendarlist.readonly): lists the owner's calendars and creates, updates or deletes events the owner has approved.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own server and stored in the owner's private notes. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • To find dates and amounts, the text of individual emails may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic's Claude API) or to a model running on the owner's own computer. Only the email being processed is sent, with no other account data.
  • Account numbers are stored as their last four digits only.

Limited use

Mail Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored notes are kept in the owner's own files and can be deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Steveshaund/mail-pipeline-site.
